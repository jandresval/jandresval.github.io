# Baskora, Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

[English](#en) · [Français](#fr) · [Español](#es)

## English {#en}

Baskora is a shopping list app made by Jaime Valencia, an individual developer. Contact: jaime.valencia.games@gmail.com.

This policy covers Baskora on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

### What the app keeps on your device

- **Your lists:** stores, products, sections, quantities, notes, prices, templates, past trips and statistics.
- **Your settings:** language, prices on or off, weekly budget.

There is no account. This data stays in the app's storage on your device and is never sent to us. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, under your phone's settings.

### What the app sends

- **Ads (Google AdMob).** The app shows ads, except in shopping mode and when you close a trip. To show and measure ads, Google's ad service receives data from your device, such as a device identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, which ads you saw or tapped, and diagnostics. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. On iPhone, the app does not ask to track you, so Google does not get your advertising identifier there. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
- **Barcode search (only when you tap Search online).** The barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to look up the product name. Nothing else is sent with it: no name, no account, no list. Open Food Facts handles requests under its own policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

The camera is used only to read barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent. Dictation uses your phone's keyboard, which is run by Apple or Google, not by Baskora.

Baskora has no analytics of its own and sells no data.

### Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete all data. Removing the app also deletes everything it kept.

### Children

Baskora is not directed at children under 13.

### Changes

If the app starts sending other data, this policy will be updated first, and the app will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

### Contact

Questions: jaime.valencia.games@gmail.com.

- **Publicités (Google AdMob).** L'application affiche des publicités, sauf en mode courses et à la fermeture d'une course.
